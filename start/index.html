Before the conversation

Four questions are enough.

  1. 01What is happening now that makes this conversation timely?
  2. 02If trying harder is not enough, what may need to change in you or in how you lead?
  3. 03What would be different if the work were useful?
  4. 04Who else is affected by the situation or has a legitimate stake in the outcome?

What happens next

01

Understand

We clarify the situation, the wider context and what is at stake.

02

Test fit

We explore whether coaching, advisory work or another route is the better response.

03

Decide

If there is a fit, we agree the next step, boundaries and working relationship.

04

Receive a clear scope

A proposed focus, format, cadence, responsibilities and fees are set out before either side commits.
