Starting point

Begin with the situation as it is.

A chemistry conversation is not a commitment to a predefined solution. It is a chance to understand the question, the context and whether working together would be useful.

Before the conversation

Three questions are enough.

  1. 01What is happening now that makes this conversation timely?
  2. 02What would be different if the work were useful?
  3. 03Who else is affected by the situation or has a legitimate stake in the outcome?

What happens next

01

Understand

We clarify the situation, the wider context and what is at stake.

02

Test fit

We explore whether coaching, advisory work or another route is the better response.

03

Decide

If there is a fit, we agree the next step, boundaries and working relationship.

Contact route

Choose a time for a chemistry conversation.

The calendar opens so you can choose a suitable time. Booking creates a first conversation—not a commitment to an engagement or a guarantee of fit.
