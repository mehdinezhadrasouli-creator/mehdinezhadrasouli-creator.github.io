Difficult Conversation Simulator for Managers

A difficult conversation is not always only a question of what to say. This is a short practice for the choices a manager makes when a real issue has already been postponed.

For managers, new leaders and people who sponsor leadership development, when a capable colleague has missed a commitment and the relationship makes the conversation easy to delay.

The situation

This situation is fictional. A capable team member has missed an agreed deadline twice. The quality of the work is generally good, but the delay is now affecting another team. You have postponed the conversation because the relationship is positive and you do not want the feedback to feel disproportionate.

What you will practice

  1. The purpose you take into the conversation
  2. How specifically you open
  3. What you do with a reasonable explanation
  4. Whether the expectation can be seen and owned
  5. What follow-through you leave in place

At the end, the debrief describes the pattern of choices in this scenario through three practice lenses: clarity under tension, curiosity without dropping the standard, and ownership of the next step. These are not validated scales.

This does not measure communication skill, personality or leadership potential. There is no pass mark and no score. It shows what the choices in this one scenario made more or less explicit.

Your choices stay in this browser session. They are not sent, stored as an assessment record, or used to build a profile.
