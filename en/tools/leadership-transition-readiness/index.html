Reflective readiness tool

Leadership Transition Readiness Check

For organizational leaders and managers navigating greater responsibility, a role transition, or a consequential decision. Twelve original questions help you notice where identity, delegation, authority, boundaries and pressure to prove yourself may be shaping the next chapter—not to grade you, but to clarify what deserves attention.

Use this if a promotion, expanded scope, or a consequential leadership decision has outpaced the habits that used to work.

This is a reflective readiness signal, not a validated psychometric test, diagnosis, hiring screen or pass/fail verdict. One difficult area does not mean you are “not ready.”

Your answers stay in this browser session only. Nothing is sent to Mehdi, stored on a server, or shared with analytics—no scores, free text or personal details.
