Evidence article

What is the Big Five? IPIP-50, scoring and limits

The Big Five is one of the most widely used descriptive frameworks for personality trait continua. This page uses Goldberg’s 50-item IPIP Big-Five Factor Markers: Extraversion, Agreeableness, Conscientiousness, Emotional Stability, and Intellect/Imagination. The result is for reflection and coaching — not diagnosis, fixed types, population percentiles or hiring decisions. Persian wording on the FA route is a reflective translation/adaptation of the public-domain English items, not a claim of exact validated Iranian research wording.

What the evidence supports

  • The five-factor structure and Goldberg-linked lexical markers are widely studied in personality psychology.
  • IPIP provides public-domain items and scoring keys; this page is not NEO-PI-R or Hogan.
  • Studies such as Gow et al. (2005), concurrent-validity reports in China, and Persian 50-item use in an Iranian student sample (Erdle & Aghababaei, 2012) support research use of this instrument family — without proving national norms or exact research wording for this page.

What it does not support

  • That results from this website implementation are population percentiles, personality types, clinical diagnoses, or hiring bases.
  • That this site’s Persian wording is the exact unpublished Iranian research translation.
  • That the tool is equivalent to proprietary NEO, Hogan, Gallup or other commercial instruments.

What is the Big Five?

The Big Five is a descriptive framework for relatively enduring tendencies in thinking, feeling and behaviour. Each factor is a continuum, not a type label.

People can score higher on some dimensions and lower on others; context and learned behaviour matter.

Which instrument does this page use?

This page uses the 50-item IPIP Big-Five Factor Markers (IPIP-50 / BFFM-50) associated with Goldberg’s lexical Big Five work.

Items and scoring logic come from public-domain IPIP sources. This page is not NEO-PI-R, Hogan or Gallup, and does not claim equivalence to proprietary instruments.

What are the five dimensions?

Labels follow the IPIP-50 lexical factor direction rather than silently swapping models.

Extraversion

Tendency toward social energy, starting conversations and active group presence — versus quietness and staying in the background.

Agreeableness

Tendency toward empathy and concern for others — versus greater emotional distance or tougher interpersonal stance.

Conscientiousness

Tendency toward order, preparedness and following through — versus more spontaneous flexibility or less structure.

Emotional Stability

Tendency toward relative calm and fewer mood swings — opposite in direction to Neuroticism in many OCEAN presentations.

Intellect / Imagination

Tendency toward ideas, imagination and abstract concepts; related to the broader Openness family without claiming a separate proprietary scale.

How does scoring work?

The response format is the standard five-point IPIP accuracy scale. +keyed items score 1→5; reverse-keyed items score 5→1.

Each factor is the sum of 10 items (raw range 10–50). An item mean from 1.00–5.00 can also be shown.

The 0–100 visual position is only a linear map of (raw − 10) / 40 × 100 — not a population percentile or norm.

What does the scientific evidence say?

Personality research offers broad support for five-factor structure and concurrent validity of IPIP markers across samples (including Gow et al., 2005, and reports in China).

In Persian/Iranian contexts, Erdle and Aghababaei (2012) used a Persian translation of the 50-item self-report IPIP with 600 student volunteers from three Tehran universities, and the IPIP translations page notes Persian translations. That does not equal national norms or prove this site’s exact wording.

More recent cautions about reverse-keyed items and cross-cultural self-report behaviour should keep interpretation modest.

Limits of the Persian wording

The exact wording of the Iranian 50-item research translation was not publicly available in the authoritative sources reviewed for this implementation.

Therefore the Persian route uses a careful translation/adaptation of the public-domain English IPIP-50 items for self-reflection, and should not be labelled as the exact independently validated Iranian research version.

The current evidence base has limits; do not treat results as Iran-wide norms or ignore reverse-wording/cross-cultural caution.

How can it be used in coaching?

Treat the profile as a reflection input for conversations about managerial role, career decisions and team relationships.

Mehdi Nejadrasouli is an ICF PCC executive/organisational coach using the result as a reflection input — not as a psychologist or psychometrician.

Useful prompts: Where does this tendency help? Where might the opposite be useful? Under pressure, how does it show up?

What does this assessment not conclude?

It does not provide clinical diagnosis, disorder labels, or mental-health screening.

It does not assign fixed personality types, or verdicts about intelligence, competence or job performance.

This website implementation is not a hiring basis, ranking tool, or population-percentile report; that limit applies to this site version and does not describe the broader IPIP research literature.

Frequently asked questions

What is the Big Five test?
A descriptive framework for five relatively enduring personality dimensions on continua. This page uses the 50-item IPIP factor markers.
Is this the same as the NEO test?
No. This page uses public-domain IPIP-50 and is not proprietary NEO-PI-R, Hogan or Gallup.
How is Emotional Stability different from Neuroticism?
On the official IPIP key, Factor IV is Emotional Stability and runs opposite to Neuroticism in many OCEAN presentations. Higher scores suggest greater relative stability — not a diagnosis.
Is the 0–100 bar a percentile?
No. It is only a visual position within the possible 10–50 raw range, not a population norm.
Is the Persian version validated?
Published work has used Persian IPIP Big Five measures, but the exact research wording was not publicly available; this site’s Persian text is a reflective adaptation and does not claim to be that exact validated wording.
Can this assessment be used for hiring?
This website implementation has not been designed or validated for hiring or ranking people and should not be used as a standalone basis for hiring, promotion, or other high-stakes employment decisions.
Where are my answers stored?
Answers are not sent to a server. They stay in this browser on this device unless you copy or share a summary yourself.
