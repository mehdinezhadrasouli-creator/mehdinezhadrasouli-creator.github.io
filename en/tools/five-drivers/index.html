What are the Five Drivers in Transactional Analysis?

In Transactional Analysis literature, drivers are patterns that help notice how behaviour can become pressured, repetitive or automatic — especially under stress.

This page is for self-awareness and conversation, not personality labelling or clinical causal claims about childhood or trauma.

What is theoretically grounded — and where are the limits?

The theoretical basis points to Kahler and Capers (1974) on the Miniscript and Kahler (1975) on drivers.

This website’s 25 statements are educational rewrites inspired by the Five Drivers model and Mary Cox’s Driver Questionnaire. They are not an official translation of a proprietary instrument, not a normed Persian psychometric test, and not an independent psychometric validation claim.

Citing the TA literature does not imply formal endorsement by those authors, or that the site wording is an official questionnaire edition.

What can each driver show?

Each driver can hold both strength and rigidity. A high score is not negative on its own; the useful coaching question is whether the pattern remains flexible and context-appropriate.

Be Perfect

Care, accuracy and standards; may become rigid perfectionism or difficulty accepting “good enough.”

Hurry Up

Pace, responsiveness and momentum; may become impatience or difficulty pausing.

Please Others

Empathy, cooperation and relational awareness; may make disagreement or boundaries harder.

Be Strong

Resilience, steadiness and self-reliance; may make vulnerability or help-seeking harder.

Try Hard

Persistence, energy and engagement; may overvalue effort or make completion and simplification harder.

How are Five Drivers scores calculated?

Each driver is the sum of five statements rated 1–5, so each driver score ranges from 5 to 25.

Relative intensity uses (score − 5) ÷ 20 to show low, moderate, high and very high bands for conversation — not diagnosis.

Is a high driver score bad?

No. A high score is not inherently bad. Each driver can be both a strength and a constraint.

Start with the two highest scores: where they help, and where they narrow choices. Small gaps usually mean several drivers are active together.

How can Five Drivers be used in coaching?

Treat the profile as a reflection starting point: where the pattern helps, and where flexibility shrinks.

Use alternative permissions as reflective prompts, not prescriptions.

If you want to explore working together, use the start path; the tool is also designed for independent self-awareness.

Limits — what it must not be used for

It is not a psychological diagnosis, clinical screening tool, or hiring instrument.

It should not be presented as a normed personality test or an official proprietary translation.

Avoid treating theoretical TA ideas about scripts or unconscious mechanisms as proven clinical facts on this page.
