Reflective tools

Assessments & Tools

A small collection of reflective tools designed to help you notice patterns, clarify priorities, and prepare for a more useful coaching conversation.

These tools support self-awareness and conversation preparation. They are not medical diagnoses and do not replace professional coaching or clinical care.

Your responses stay in this browser on this device. Nothing is sent to Mehdi or stored on a server. These are reflective coaching tools, not medical, psychological or diagnostic assessments.

  • Grounded in PWI-A · 15–20 minutes · stays on this device

    Wheel of Life

    A current snapshot across important areas of life, followed by reflection on where attention or action may be useful.

    Open tool →

  • Reflective · 10–15 minutes · stays on this device

    Five Drivers Reflection

    Twenty-five statements for noticing Five Drivers behavioural patterns and preparing a coaching conversation — not a diagnosis and not for hiring.

    Open tool →

  • IPIP-50 · 12–18 minutes · stays on this device

    Big Five Personality (IPIP-50)

    Fifty public-domain IPIP statements for a five-dimension continuum profile, with transparent scoring, local-only results, PDF and coaching reflection — not a diagnosis and not a percentile.

    Open tool →

  • Reflective tool · 8–12 minutes · answers are not submitted

    Organizational Coaching Readiness Checklist

    Twelve evidence-informed questions on goals, authority, manager support, confidentiality and transfer conditions before organizational coaching begins.

    Open tool →

  • 2026 research · 5–8 minutes · no name or email

    Leadership Coaching Transfer Pulse 2026

    An open, no-name practitioner research survey on what helps executive and organizational coaching transfer into observable workplace behaviour.
