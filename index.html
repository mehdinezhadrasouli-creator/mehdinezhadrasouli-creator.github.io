A point of view

People questions are rarely only people questions.

A leadership problem may also be a role-design problem. A performance problem may also be a system, incentive or capability problem.

Coaching brings depth to the individual conversation. Organizational experience keeps the wider field visible.

Evidence before amplification.

A knowledge system for preserving the context behind an idea before AI helps extend it into public assets.

  1. 01EvidencePractice, professional experience, research, reflection and source material.
  2. 02KnowledgeWhat the evidence helps us understand, with its context and limits preserved.
  3. 03PatternsRecurring observations that are useful enough to test across situations.
  4. 04FrameworksSimple structures that clarify a decision, distinction or course of action.
  5. 05AssetsArticles, field notes, questions, tools and learning resources that can be reused.
