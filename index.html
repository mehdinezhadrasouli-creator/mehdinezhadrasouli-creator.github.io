Coaching & Leadership

9 min read

The client chooses the direction; the coach helps keep it visible

Client-led coaching does not mean leaving the process unattended. The client owns the topic, meaning and decisions; the coach helps hold the agreement, focus and changes of direction in partnership.

Evidence: Reflective practice + ICF standard

Coaching & Leadership

6 min read

When you cannot change the system, where can you start?

Not everything is under our control. Coaching can help separate what is within a leader’s control from what they can influence, and turn a large problem into choices and actions that are still available.

Evidence: Coaching practice + organizational experience

Coaching in Organizations

11 min read

Organizational coaching starts with the contract, not the first session

When an organization sponsors coaching, the challenge is not choosing between confidentiality and accountability. It is designing an agreement that protects the client's thinking while making the purpose and observable outcomes clear.

Evidence: Practice + research
