Leadership transitions and difficult decisions

Context
Senior managers carrying a larger role, more ambiguity or a decision that cannot usefully be made by someone else.
Work
Confidential executive coaching that keeps authorship with the leader while making the role, relationships and organizational context visible.
Evidence base

02

Organizational coaching that can transfer into work

Context
Organizations want development connected to a real role without turning confidential coaching into a reporting channel.
Work
Multi-party contracting, observable goals, protected confidentiality, manager or sponsor support, and review points built around what can be seen in real work.
Evidence base
This practice architecture is documented across the organizational coaching service and public writing on manager role, transfer, measurement and readiness.
