Executive coaching

A confidential thinking space for leaders carrying real consequence.

Executive coaching is one-to-one professional coaching for a leader whose role, decisions or relationships now demand a different quality of thinking. The work starts with the live situation, not with a packaged programme.

What is this work?

Executive coaching is a confidential conversation with a professional coach, contracted around a leadership question that matters. The coach does not take over the decision. The aim is clearer thinking, more honest choice and action the leader can own.

Mehdi works as an ICF PCC. The coaching conversation stays with the person, while the role, relationships and organizational context remain visible so the work does not become abstract.

Who it is for

  • 01Executives and senior managers whose scope, visibility or ambiguity has increased.
  • 02Leaders facing a consequential decision that is entangled with identity, relationships or politics.
  • 03Leaders who need a protected place to think, not a ready-made answer on their behalf.

When it is relevant

  • 01The role has changed, but the old answers no longer fit.
  • 02A difficult conversation, boundary or decision keeps being postponed.
  • 03The leader can see the problem, yet the next move still will not land.
  • 04Influence is required in a system the leader cannot fully redesign.

What it is not

  • 01You want the coach to make the decision or provide a ready-made answer on the leader's behalf.
  • 02It is not mentoring as a defined public service, and it is not a replacement for therapy, legal advice or specialist expertise outside the agreed scope.
  • 03It is not a reporting channel for the organization, even when the leader’s development is sponsored.

How the engagement starts

The first step is a 30-minute discovery conversation. The aim is fit and clarity: what is happening, what you are trying to change, and whether coaching is the useful form of work.

If there is a fit, the next step is a written outline of purpose, format, likely cadence, responsibilities and fees. Booking a discovery conversation does not enrol anyone in a programme.

Confidentiality

The coaching conversation is confidential. If an organization is involved, objectives and the shape of progress can be agreed with stakeholders, while the content of sessions remains protected.

Why this practice

Mehdi is an ICF Professional Certified Coach with more than 700 professional coaching hours. The coaching work is also shaped by more than 15 years in HR and people leadership across industrial, FMCG, digital and financial-services environments.

That combination matters when a leadership question is not only personal. The conversation can stay with the person without losing the system around them.

Questions before we begin

What is a discovery conversation?
A first conversation to understand the situation, explore what you are trying to change and see whether working together makes sense. The aim is fit and clarity, not an early sales decision.
Is coaching confidential?
Yes. The coaching conversation is confidential. In organizational engagements, objectives and the shape of progress can be agreed with stakeholders, while the content of coaching sessions remains protected.
How many sessions does an engagement usually involve?
There is no fixed package before the situation is understood. After the discovery conversation, the proposed scope sets out the purpose, likely cadence, review points and boundaries so you can decide with clarity before committing.
