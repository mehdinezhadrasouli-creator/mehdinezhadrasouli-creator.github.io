BEFORE YOU DECIDE

Questions worth asking first

A discovery conversation does not obligate you to begin a coaching program.

Are coaching conversations confidential?

Yes. In organizational engagements, goals and ways to review progress are agreed, but the content of individual coaching conversations is not reported to the sponsor.

How do I know whether coaching fits this situation?

Discovery helps clarify the situation and intended outcomes. If the issue calls for expert recommendations rather than coaching, advisory work may be a better fit. Any change in role requires explicit agreement.

What happens after the discovery conversation?

If it makes sense to continue, we agree on the focus, session format, responsibilities and fees in a clear proposal. Booking a discovery conversation does not commit you to a coaching package.
