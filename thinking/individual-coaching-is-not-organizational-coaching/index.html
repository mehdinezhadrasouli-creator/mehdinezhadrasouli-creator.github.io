Coaching in Organizations

Individual coaching is not organizational coaching

When an organization sponsors coaching, the work needs two things at once: protected confidentiality and explicit connection to organizational purpose.

22 August 20266 min readPractice distinction

Core question

How can coaching serve the client without losing the organizational context?

Evidence note

A working distinction developed through organizational and executive coaching practice. It describes Mehdi's approach and is not presented as a universal model.

01

More than a second person paying the invoice

In individual coaching, the client and the person commissioning the work are often the same. In organizational coaching, they may not be. A sponsor can have a legitimate interest in why the engagement exists, while the leader needs a genuinely confidential space in which to think.

If this difference is left implicit, expectations drift. The sponsor may expect reporting. The client may fear surveillance. The coach may be pulled into managing both sides after the work has already begun.

02

Confidentiality is designed, not improvised

A useful contract makes the boundaries visible before the first substantive session: who the client is, who the sponsor is, what outcomes matter, what can be shared, what remains confidential and how progress will be reviewed.

The content of coaching conversations remains protected. At the same time, the engagement does not float free of the real role, relationships and outcomes that gave rise to it.

03

Work at both levels

The leader may need to change how they decide, communicate or lead. The organization may also need to clarify the role, align stakeholders or remove a structural contradiction. Organizational coaching keeps both possibilities available.

The purpose is not to make the client carry a system problem alone. It is to locate responsibility accurately enough for change to hold.

Questions to carry forward

  1. 01Have the client, sponsor and coach agreed what success means?
  2. 02Is everyone clear about what can and cannot be shared?
  3. 03What must change around the leader for the development to be sustainable?
