Short bio

Mehdi Nejadrasouli, ICF PCC, is an executive and organizational coach and senior HR leader with 17 years of HR experience and more than 700 hours of professional coaching experience.

Extended bio

His work focuses on leadership transitions, organizational coaching, strategic HR and the point where individual development meets the wider system around the leader. His HR career spans industrial, FMCG, digital and financial-services environments.

In public writing and research, he separates source evidence from practitioner judgment and uses de-identified patterns from professional practice rather than client-identifiable coaching content.

Topics for editors and publications

Topics I can contribute articles, interviews or expert commentary on.

If you edit or write for an HR, leadership or coaching publication, these are areas where I can contribute a sourced article, an interview perspective or practitioner commentary.

Organizational coaching that transfers into work

Goals, sponsor and manager roles, confidentiality, feedback, practice opportunities and the system conditions that help behaviour change show up on the job.

Leadership transitions and newly promoted leaders

Role identity, decision rights, delegation, pressure after promotion and the shift from individual contribution to leading through others.

When a people problem is also a system problem

How structure, incentives, authority and management context can reinforce the behaviour an organization is trying to coach or develop.

Succession, internal mobility and talent retention

Career visibility, readiness criteria, manager behaviour and the organizational conditions that affect whether strong people stay, move or leave.

Feedback and difficult leadership conversations

Why capable managers delay important conversations, how clarity and respect can coexist, and what makes feedback usable rather than merely delivered.

Responsible use of AI in professional coaching

Using AI to amplify professional judgment and documented experience without fabricating evidence, flattening confidentiality or replacing the human coaching relationship.
